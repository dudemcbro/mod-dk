Player guide: leveling a Death Knight from 1
===========================================

On a server with this module, a Death Knight starts at level 1 and levels like
any other class: you learn your abilities from a trainer as you go, and your
damage grows with you instead of starting at full strength.

Your server admin can turn parts of this off, so if something here doesn't
match what you see, ask them.

Before you play: the client patch
---------------------------------
Your server gives you a file called `patch-d.MPQ` (it may have another letter,
like `patch-e.MPQ`). It lets you runeforge low level weapons and makes your
spell tooltips show the right levels and damage.

1. Close the game.
2. Copy the file into the `Data` folder of your WoW 3.3.5a install, next to
   `common.MPQ`.
3. Delete the `Cache` folder in your WoW folder.
4. Start the game.

Without it you can still play, but runeforging fails with "Target is too low
level" and tooltips show the wrong numbers.

Creating your Death Knight
--------------------------
Create a Death Knight as usual; on most servers running this module you don't
need a level 55 character first. You start at level 1 in Acherus and watch
the Death Knight intro. When it ends (or you skip it), a message tells you
where your trainer is and you're sent to your race's starting zone. Your
hearthstone is set there.

If you log out during the intro, you'll be sent to your starting zone the next
time you log in.

Your starting kit
-----------------
- Rusted Chain gloves, vest, leggings and boots, a Gray Woolen Shirt, a
  Bastard Sword (two-handed) and a Hearthstone.
- One ability: **Plague Strike**, in the second action bar slot next to Attack.
- Armor: cloth, leather and mail. Plate comes at level 40 from your trainer.
- Weapons: two-handed only at first. Learn one-handed weapon skills from a
  weapon master like any other class, and Dual Wield from your trainer at 20.
- First Aid is learned from a First Aid trainer like everyone else.

Finding your trainer
--------------------
Your trainer is **Madness, Death Knight Trainer**. Madness stands right next
to the priest trainer, with a runeforge beside them, in:

- **Starting zones:** Northshire, Coldridge Valley, Shadowglen, Ammen Vale,
  Valley of Trials, Deathknell, Sunstrider Isle and Camp Narache
- **Capital cities:** Stormwind (Cathedral), Ironforge (Mystic Ward),
  Darnassus (Temple of the Moon), the Exodar, Orgrimmar (Valley of Spirits),
  the Undercity, Thunder Bluff (Spirit Rise) and Silvermoon City

Your server may have placed more. Training costs gold, about what a warrior
pays.

| 30-38 | Strangulate (30), Pestilence, Death and Decay rank 2 (32), Plague and Blood Strike rank 4 (34), Death Strike rank 2, Rune of the Fallen Crusader, Forceful Deflection (36), Icy Touch rank 3 (38) |
| 40-48 | Icebound Fortitude, Runes of Swordbreaking and Swordshattering, Unholy Presence, Acherus Deathcharger, Rune Strike, Plate Mail (40), Anti-Magic Shell, Obliterate (44), Death and Decay rank 3, Plague and Blood Strike rank 5 (46), Empower Rune Weapon (48) |
| 50-60 | Blood Boil rank 3 (50), Death Strike rank 3 (52), Icy Touch rank 4, Rune of the Nerubian Carapace (54), Rune of the Stoneskin Gargoyle, Rune of Cinderglacier, Death Gate (55), Plague and Blood Strike rank 6 (56), Rune of Spellshattering (57), Death Coil rank 2, Rune of Spellbreaking (58), Army of the Dead, Death and Decay rank 4 (60) |

From 60 to 80 you learn the normal Death Knight abilities at their normal
levels. Abilities aren't learned automatically: visit Madness when you level.

Why your damage starts low
--------------------------
A Death Knight's abilities are built for level 55, so at low levels the
server scales them down to keep you in line with other classes. The scaling
fades as you level and is gone at **level 55**. It covers your spells, your
talent abilities, your heals and your pets (ghoul, Army of the Dead and so on).
Your plain weapon swings are never reduced, and your strikes keep their full
weapon damage; only their bonus damage is scaled.

With the client patch, your tooltips show the scaled numbers for your level.

Presences, tanking and talents
------------------------------
- **Blood Presence** (level 10), **Frost Presence** (18) and **Unholy
  Presence** (40). Blood and Frost Presence also scale up to full strength by
  level 40.
- Below level 30, your spells cause extra threat while you're in Blood or
  Frost Presence, so you can hold enemies in early dungeons. Icy Touch, Blood
  Boil and Death and Decay are your best threat tools.
- You get talent points from level 10, like every class.

Runeforging
-----------
At level 20, learn **Runeforging** and **Rune of Razorice** from Madness. Then
stand at a runeforge (there's one next to every Madness), and cast the rune on
your weapon. Runes work on low level weapons and take effect right away. A
rune binds the weapon to you. You learn more runes as you level (see the table
above).

Mount, Death Gate and Ebon Hold
-------------------------------
- **Acherus Deathcharger** at level 40; learn riding at a normal riding trainer.
- **Death Gate** at level 55 takes you to Ebon Hold and back.
- Below level 55 you can leave Ebon Hold freely, including with your
  hearthstone.

Playing the Ebon Hold story later
---------------------------------
Because you skipped the Death Knight starting story, you can play it later.
At level 55 or higher, talk to the **Ebon Herald** at Light's Hope Chapel in
the Eastern Plaguelands. They explain what happens and ask you to confirm.
Then:

- you're sent to Acherus to start the story from its first quest,
- your earlier Ebon Hold quest progress starts over,
- you keep your talents, gear and gold,
- you're given Death Gate if you don't have it, so you can leave whenever you
  want.

Your quest log needs a free slot. Once you finish the story, the Ebon Herald
has nothing more to offer you.

Common questions
----------------
**"Target is too low level" when I runeforge.** Install the client patch and
delete your `Cache` folder (see [above](#before-you-play-the-client-patch)).

**I keep seeing "You have learned" and "You have unlearned" when I level.**
That's a server setup problem; tell your admin.

**My tooltips don't match my damage.** Make sure you have the latest patch file
from your server. Tooltips also can't show the scaling for talent abilities
and pets.

**Where's my trainer?** Next to the priest trainer in any starting zone or
capital city (see [Finding your trainer](#finding-your-trainer)).
